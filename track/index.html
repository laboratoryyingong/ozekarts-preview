02 — Track tips

Faster
every lap.

01Brake before the turnSlow down before entering the corner, keeping your kart straight while braking. Turn in smoothly to maintain control and carry speed through the bend.
02Apply power smoothlyThe track's low-grip concrete makes throttle control essential. Apply power gradually as you exit the corner and straighten the wheel to maintain traction.
03Look where you want to goFocus on the path ahead and aim for the corner exit rather than the barriers. Looking ahead helps you steer more smoothly and confidently.
04Master the slideDrifting is part of the fun, but smooth cornering can help you achieve faster lap times. Experiment with both techniques and compare your recorded times to see what works best for you.
